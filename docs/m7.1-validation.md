## Regression evidence

The M7.1 tests exercise different `go depth 8`/`go depth 3` commands across both
paired colors, mixed depth/movetime/clock configurations, integer/range validation,
exact outgoing transcripts compared with the mock's received-command files,
session/game/attempt/color/ply attribution and monotonically ordered captures.
They verify reported depth differs from requested depth, missing/malformed
counters are absent, actual zero remains zero, PV/string tokens do not become
counters, raw info retention is bounded, and entry/byte exhaustion is explicitly
truncated. Cooperative stop produces interrupted evidence; failed searches do
not become accepted moves or complete captures. A native Chess Lab search verifies
real depth/nodes and outgoing commands.

Legacy manifests, attempts without new fields, PGN, idempotent resume and immutable
manifest bytes are covered. Incomplete attempts export only durable checkpoints;
corrupt checkpoints/finals fail closed. The additional integrity test tampers
with trace bytes and changes an argv artifact while capture is enabled. The
full existing suite also retains receipt/deadline/stop-grace tests, identity drift,
immutable publication, interrupted/torn recovery, durable-ACK failure and
post-rename write-uncertainty regressions without relaxed assertions.

Protocol capture is bounded per attempt and disabled by default. It observes
submitted stdin commands and complete stdout lines during attempts, excluding
preflight inspections, stderr and unterminated fragments. It cannot prove an
engine consumed a submitted command. A crash can lose active-search evidence
since the last durable move checkpoint; incomplete exports explicitly say so.
Standard UCI provides no independent proof a requested depth completed, or why
an engine internally ended a timed search. Completion labels refer to the UCI
exchange and controller stop/failure observations. Reported statistics remain
engine-specific and are not normalized or independently certified.

## Review fixes: observer failures and long experiments

The follow-up changes are confined to `controller/src/engine.ts`,
`controller/src/diagnostics.ts`, `controller/src/experiment.ts`,
`controller/test/m7.1-experiment-controls.test.mjs`, `controller/README.md` and
this report. Prior GUI, engine-limit and protocol-fixture changes are preserved.

`ManagedEngine` still catches protocol-observer exceptions and continues normal
engine operations. A sticky `protocolObserverFailures` count now prevents
`protocolComplete` from becoming true after any observation failure, including
handshake, incoming search output and quit. Exported protocol metadata includes
the aggregate `observerFailures` count and cannot claim complete coverage when
that count is nonzero. Ordinary search completion remains distinct from failed
diagnostics. Regressions inject one failing observation at each lifecycle stage,
execute two legal moves, and verify subsequent successful observation does not
restore completeness. An experiment-level regression deliberately throws inside
the collector and checks accepted moves plus incomplete persisted/exported traces.

The selected long-experiment strategy is bounded detail with streaming aggregate
statistics. A new `SearchEvidenceCapture` retains an ordered prefix of at most
256 per-search records and 262144 serialized entry bytes per attempt by default.
Optional JSON `searchEvidence: {"maxEntries":256,"maxBytes":262144}` allows
1..4096 records and 1..4194304 bytes. Absent configuration does not add a field to
old manifests, and existing completed results are never rewritten. Reaching a
budget affects diagnostic detail only; engine commands, game clocks, adjudication
and `maxPlies` do not change.

After saturation the collector stops retaining/serializing candidate raw records
and updates only constant-size per-engine counts, completion/interruption/failure
counts, measured elapsed totals and reported-field sample counts/ranges. Missing
optional counters remain unavailable. The growing session-to-ply map is replaced
by one current attribution per engine process. `searches` retains full original
attribution for its prefix; `searchEvidence` explicitly reports limits, retained
bytes/count, observed/dropped counts, truncation, first/last omitted attribution
and aggregate summaries covering all observed searches. It does not imply the
omitted per-move detail can be reconstructed. Checkpoints always report incomplete
lifecycle coverage; final detail coverage is complete only when nothing was omitted.

Export merges these all-search aggregates without rebuilding unbounded flattened
search arrays, exposes retained/dropped counts, and continues to support earlier
M7.1 array-only and M6 statistic-free records. Evidence and aggregates stay inside
the existing checksummed/fsynced state and immutable final record. There are no
new sidecar write barriers or recovery paths. Interrupted attempts retain their
last durable coverage; a restarted attempt gets a new collector from its opening.

The added diagnostic memory/checkpoint payload is bounded independently of
`maxPlies`. Rewriting this bounded prefix has linear total serialization cost in
the number of searches after saturation. Authoritative move/event history still
grows and retains its preexisting checkpoint costs; this fix does not claim
constant-size whole-game records or a bound on the total multi-attempt export.

Focused tests feed **100000 synthetic searches**, check retained entry/byte
bounds and checkpoint payload size, verify exact aggregates for every observation,
and assert later omitted raw records are not serialized. These are collector
stress observations, not a claim to have played a 100000-ply chess game. Mock
paired-game tests force both entry and byte exhaustion in actual checkpoints and
exports, inspect checksums, check attribution and all-search statistics, tamper
with aggregate bytes to ensure integrity rejection, verify immutable/idempotent
resume, and recover a bounded interrupted checkpoint into fresh attempts.
